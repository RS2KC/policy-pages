Privacy Policy

This application is a personal mail-organizing program with a single user, its owner, who runs it on their own computer. This policy describes what it does with the information it can reach through Google.

What it accesses

The application uses the Gmail API to read the messages in one mailbox, an account created for it, and to create drafts in that mailbox. It sends email only to its owner's own addresses. It does not access any other Google account, contacts, calendar or files.

How the information is used

Messages are read so that the application can file them by project, note what they ask for, and prepare drafts for the owner to approve. Nothing is sent, posted or changed anywhere without the owner's approval, given in the application's own interface.

Where it is stored

Everything the application keeps is stored in an encrypted database on the owner's computer. Before any text is sent to a third-party text-processing service, names, addresses, phone numbers and other identifying details are replaced by placeholders; the real values never leave the owner's computer and are restored only there.

Sharing

The application does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. The placeholder text sent to third-party processing services is used only to produce the application's output for its owner and is not used to train those services' models where the service offers that choice.

Retention and deletion

Information stays on the owner's computer for as long as the owner keeps it. The owner can delete the application's database at any time, and can revoke its access to the Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the app's owner, at the support address shown on the Google sign-in screen for the application.
