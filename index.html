Ari

A personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer.

Ari is a private, single-user application. It reads the mail of a mailbox set up for it, keeps track of projects and the tasks that come out of them, and drafts follow-ups and status updates for its owner to review. Nothing is sent, changed or ordered without the owner's explicit approval.

How it uses Google

Ari signs in to one Google account, its own, to read the messages sent to that mailbox and to save drafts there. It does not read any other account, does not share what it reads with anyone, and does not send email to anyone but its owner.

Where the data lives

Everything Ari keeps is stored in an encrypted database on the owner's own Mac. Names and other identifying details are replaced by placeholders before any text is sent to a language-model service for analysis, and put back only on the owner's machine.

See the privacy policy.

Ari is not offered to the public. It has one user: its owner.
