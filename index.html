A private assistant application

This page describes a personal software application that its owner runs on their own computer. It is not offered to the public and has one user: its owner.

What it does

The application reads the mail of one mailbox set up for it, keeps track of the owner's projects and tasks, and prepares drafts for the owner to review. Nothing is sent, changed or ordered without the owner's explicit approval.

How it uses Google

It signs in to one Google account, its own, to read the messages sent to that mailbox and to save drafts there. It reads no other account, shares nothing it reads with anyone, and sends email to nobody but its owner.

Where the data lives

Everything it keeps is stored in an encrypted database on the owner's own computer. Identifying details are replaced by placeholders before any text is sent to a language-model service for analysis, and put back only on the owner's machine.
